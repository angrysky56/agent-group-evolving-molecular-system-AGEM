# Quantum no-go constraints: n-ary extraction fixture

Each paragraph states one corpus-level constraint. The intended claim is the
complete jointly incompatible set, not a set of pairwise exclusions and not a
claim that any one member is false by itself.

## Bell

No model can simultaneously satisfy locality, realism, measurement
independence, and empirical adequacy for the observed Bell-inequality
violations.

## Kochen-Specker

No model can simultaneously satisfy noncontextual value definiteness,
functional consistency across compatible measurements, and empirical adequacy
for the Kochen-Specker configurations.

## PBR

No model can simultaneously satisfy preparation independence, overlapping
psi-epistemic ontic distributions for distinct pure states, and empirical
adequacy for the PBR measurement statistics.

## Frauchiger-Renner

No position can simultaneously hold universal validity of quantum theory,
single-world outcomes, consistency between agents' conclusions, and the
self-referential multi-agent protocol assumptions used by Frauchiger-Renner.

## Leggett-Garg

No model can simultaneously satisfy macrorealism per se, non-invasive
measurability, induction, and empirical adequacy for a Leggett-Garg inequality
violation.

## Brukner

No position can simultaneously hold observer-independent facts, freedom of
choice, locality, universal validity of quantum theory, and empirical adequacy
for the extended Wigner's-friend correlations in Brukner's theorem.
